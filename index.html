How to read this

What is R?
The amount risked on one trade. +2R made twice the risk. A full stop-out is about one R lost. R works for any account size; dollars flatter big ones.
Is this real money?
The badge at the top says which. PAPER is a simulated account with fees and slippage included. When real money starts, the badge changes and the two records are never mixed.
Are losers shown?
Every trade counts, from the first one. Each entry is posted to the Tape channel before its outcome is known and is never edited.
Is this a signal service?
No. Nothing is for sale and nothing here is advice. It is a lab notebook kept in public.
